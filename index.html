Behaviour States

Find what you are seeing and follow the action.

Settled Lying still or dozing. Soft eyes, slow breathing. May look up but stays where she is.

→ Leave her to rest undisturbed.

Playful Wagging, bouncing, bringing you a toy. Responds when you speak to her and pauses when you pause.

→ Join in or offer a toy. Keep the pace easy.

Ramping Up Jumping repeatedly, mouthing hands or clothes, barking at you, not stopping when play pauses.

→ Stop the activity. Step back, turn away, and wait quietly until she settles before re-engaging.

Unsettled Pacing, whining, or sniffing repeatedly near the door. Can't stay still but not excited about play.

→ Offer a short toilet break outside. If pacing continues, try crate rest with a chew.

Care Quick Reference

Check here before doing anything for the first time.

Feeding

Morning · ~8 am ¾ cup dry
Evening · ~5 pm ¾ cup dry

Do

  • Fresh water with every meal
  • Toilet break 15 min after eating
  • Use her name calmly when calling

Avoid

  • Human food and table scraps
  • Play once she starts ramping up
  • Scolding after calling her to you
